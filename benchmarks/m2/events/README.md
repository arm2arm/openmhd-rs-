# Transient DE face in the 69-site S20 fixture

The registered event family is specified in [the moving-stage contract](../../../docs/galaxy/MOVING_MHD_STAGE_SPEC.md#91-concrete-transient-face-fixture-inputs). Each job converts the exact design to binary64 once, retains its 69 original position/velocity words and builds the reference from their exact dyadic values. Cases cover three depths, three shifts, all six coordinate permutations and both boundary/time profiles. Each receives one CPU and 4 GiB. A first periodic short-duration certificate checks the implementation before release of the family.

Four stationary generators A, B, C and D define the circumcentre O. Write G with rows A−D, B−D and C−D. With normalized time s, compute α from Gᵀα=E(s)−D and F(s)=|E(s)−O|²−|D−O|². The signs of the three affine coefficients α are checked over the full interval using exact endpoint bounds. For α>0 the distances to A, B and C constrain z=G(X−O) to the negative octant. The DE bisector cuts it at 2α·z=F. This intersection is empty for F>0, a point for F=0, and a triangle with intercepts F/(2αᵢ) for F<0.

Each certificate encloses the entire family of triangles between the two actual roots. Against this enclosure it checks every other seed and potentially nearest periodic image along its complete linear path. The triangle stays strictly inside the unit box. Roots and the three interior comparison polygons are enclosed with exact fractions and 384-bit outward square-root brackets. Rigid axis permutations and the two boundary/time profiles each retain their own numerical input. The outputs establish event times and face presence for the reference. A moving solver must still visit the event on its own physical schedule and measure its exchanges.
