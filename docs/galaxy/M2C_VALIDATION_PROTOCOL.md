# M2c validation of the periodic moving-mesh reference

## Magnetic mean at initialization

The moving-stage specification (§10, S20a) requires that the actual volume-weighted periodic field agree with the configured mean to within 1e-9 of the fixed initial magnetic scale, including at initialization. This is a prerequisite for time integration. Test the selected `periodic-ls-quadratic-point-curl-v1` using every original-field O02 configuration underlying F01. Use all four accepted regular and jitter families, both imposed means, and N = 8, 16, 32 from the M2b revision 2 archive. This produces 24 separate REANA workflows on existing native arrays. The unequal-wave-number regular control is a distinct field and is outside F01.

For each component compute `sum_i(V_i B_ik)/sum_i V_i - Bmean_k`, using the original Rust quadratic field, original volumes and a compensated sum. Record signed components, the maximum absolute component, its Euclidean norm and the ratio of the maximum component to the original Fourier field scale of 2π in the unit box. Use the component criterion of 1e-9. Also report the component ratio for the deliberately conservative analytical upper bound `2 sqrt(3) (2π) + |Bmean|`. Record arithmetic noise using independently ordered summation and exact rational arithmetic over the recorded binary64 field and volume words. A narrower fitted field, a measured offset or a different curl cannot supply a pass. Keep an unsuccessful scientific verdict in a successfully collected workflow output. A failed initialization precludes coupled time evolution of this candidate. Independent moving-geometry and tangent checks can still be measured.

## Periodic point-curl derivative (S-CDOT)

Use the accepted quadratic point-curl and the actual S-CENTROID geometry rates. Hold each real owner's three field samples fixed while a smooth periodic Voronoi geometry changes. Compare analytically differentiated coefficients with central differences of the original assembled static curl on separately rebuilt endpoints. The dyadic unit cube has 27 sites on a 3×3×3 product lattice. In axis k, coordinate j has offset `((j+2*k)%3-1)/64` from the cell-centred lattice. Freeze each generator velocity to the component table `((j+k)%3-1)/16`; the product deformation preserves the face inventory while varying cell volumes, centroid separations and quadratic fit weights. Prescribe the same velocity for every generator in each coordinate plane. Use spacings 1/16 through 1/1024, halving six times. Check the full owner/image stencil inventory against the base epoch. Exact field words in the nonpolynomial case come from the owner index by the formula in the integration test. Report worst fixed-sample derivative errors, separately scaled by max(1, maximum tangent component). Require adjacent order at least 1.8 above the 1e-11 roundoff floor and finest scaled error at most 1e-7.

Independently verify the moving-sample chain rule for all linear and quadratic polynomial directions on a full-rank lifted stencil with an affine velocity map. Check the column-normalization and physical-length derivative, constant gauge modes, rigid motion, velocity/wall identity and epoch handling. Compare the Euclidean transpose and both the static and tangent volume-weighted diagonals to explicit basis probes, including coalescing multiple images of one real owner. Record local QR condition estimates and inverse sensitivities separately from inverse-curl claims. Use 1e-12 scaled algebra tolerance. One independent case per REANA workflow, 1 CPU and 4 GiB, following frozen strict-Clippy, format and locked release compilation. The bounded constrained curl has a separate definition.

## Dependencies for milestone closure

After S-CDOT, M01 checks prescribed translations, windings and smooth volume change. M02 requires finite-step geometric conservation and event-resolved physical face exchanges. Moving-field and coupled tests then cover field advection, mean preservation, 3D waves, conservation, boosts and restart, blasts and vortices. The source-linked thresholds remain those of the [moving-stage specification](MOVING_MHD_STAGE_SPEC.md) and [3D mesh plan](MESH_3D_SPEC.md).
